Blocks Sounds

Every block in data/blocks.toml, drawn texel for texel by the game's own recipe. Pick one, change its look or its sound, hear it, and give it a verdict. Copy my review collects everything from both benches to paste to Claude; nothing here changes the game by itself.

Models. Every block is a full cube today and the game has no other models yet: no player body, tools, machines or creatures. Shapes smaller than a block come with the 12.5 cm fine cells, eight to a block's edge. When the first models exist they get a bench here. Colours are shown before the game's sun, shadow and haze.

Every sound in data/sounds.toml, made here by the same recipe the game uses, sample for sample. The triangle plays take 1; pick a sound to hear its other takes, hear it as in the game (a random take, a little louder or softer, higher or lower), and change its layers.
