Block Bench

Every block in data/blocks.toml, drawn texel for texel by the game's own recipe: the cube, its top and side flat, and three blocks of wall to see how it repeats. Change a colour, a pattern or a number and the picture follows. Give each a verdict, then Copy my review and paste it to Claude. Nothing here changes the game by itself. Colours are shown before the game's sun, shadow and haze.

Models. Every block is a full cube today, and the game has no other models yet: no player body, tools, machines or creatures. Shapes smaller than a block (slabs, stairs, chiselled pieces) come with the 12.5 cm fine cells, eight to a block's edge. When the first models exist they get a shelf here.
