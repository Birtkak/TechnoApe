- Bots (`technoape-bot`): `look dx dy dz` looks at the middle of the block there until the next walk (the server judges the journal's `look:` from it twice a second); `report` carries `"journal":{"flint":"known",..}` (every entry not unknown). `drink dx dy dz` holds the use button on the water there for 2.5 s; `report` carries
  `"vitals":[health,hunger,thirst,breath]`, `"death"` and `"home"` the centres of its death pile's cell and its sleeping place (null without); `use dx dy dz` on a bed claims it. `prospect dx dy dz` strikes the prospecting tool in hand on that block and prints the answer
  as one JSON line, `{"bot":..,"prospect":[["copper","strong"],..]}` (empty: nothing in range); `hold k` first.
  Crafting as a player does: the grid's cells are slots 37 to 45 (a 3 x 3 row by row; 37, 38, 40, 41 the 2 x 2),
  filled by `move a b n`; `craft [n]` takes the output n times (1 if left out); `use dx dy dz` uses the block there
  (a workbench opens its 3 x 3), `close` closes the screen (the cells back into the pack); `slots` after a
  `wait 0.5` shows what came of it. A crucible (ADR 0141): `use dx dy dz` opens it, `put a dx dy dz k n` and
  `take dx dy dz k a n` at its cell reach its slots (0 in, 1 fuel, 2 mould, 3 out, 4 the second input for an alloy),
  `cast dx dy dz <name>` chooses a cast's shape by the name of any row of it (`cast dx dy dz` alone none), `furnace` prints the open one's slots, heat, fire and work as one JSON line.
- (Tune it by numbers with `technoape-studio tune=-`, then `hand`, `set snap 3`, `save`; ADR 0171.) The hand's swing: `data/hand.toml` holds its numbers (length, curve, reach, aim, chop, the bare arm's pose; ADR 0172) and a running game reads it again within a second of a change.
- Looking at something (`run=stand`, CLAUDE.md): `weather=clear` (or another `/weather` name) forces the weather from
  the start, `creatures=0` makes the local server keep no animals (no herd is made, live data is off, no save is read or written), so a fixed
  viewpoint is the same picture however long its load took (both on every line of `tests/views/views.txt`).
  `depth=m` stands m metres under the ground, in the rock (a new world's grid goes 1248 m down through the
  mantle, ADR 0152): `place=` with `air` digs the room around the stand cell, lamps light it, e.g.
  `run=stand depth=1000 place=0:0:0:air,0:0:1:air,0:0:2:air,1:0:1:lamp,...`; `floor=k` instead stands on the
  bottom of layer k (`floor=3 place=...` with `air` down to `0:0:-3` opens the floor onto the metal sea). `mantle=0` (the game, the server and
  its `report=1`) makes a new world on the 192 m floor of the worlds from before.
- `gen=k` (the game, the server and `report=1`): a new world of generation k (ADR
  0160-generation-number-and-arc-basalt): 0 the worlds before the mantle (as `mantle=0`), 1 the mantle's (ADR 0154,
  0159), 2 (arcs' basalt flows, round lava tubes, only copper placed for where the spawn is), 3 (karst), 4 (Luna's
  rock field), 5 (Mars, the third body), 6 (the gas giant, the fourth), 7 (the giant's icy moon, the
  fifth), 8 (real gravity), 9 the newest (every body with blocks has the deep grid down to its core: Luna over a metal
  sea, Mars over a closed floor of iron-nickel, Callisto of stone). A world keeps
  its generation in its save and sends it to clients and bots; `report=1` prints it as `gen`. For tests and for
  comparing one seed's worlds; a new world without it is the newest.
- Mars (generation 5): `/goto mars` lands on its day side; `run=stand body=2 at=x,y,z` stands on it (`body=0` Terra,
  `1` Luna), `look=Mars` (or `look=Terra`, `look=Luna`) faces that body's centre from where you stand; `report=1`
  prints a `mars` section (its kinds' shares, heights, the tubes its map marks). Its ground loads when you near
  it (from a pixel across, on a thread of its own): `/goto mars` says "going to Mars: its ground is loading" and
  takes you there when it is in; flying in, you are held where you are, your speed kept, 600 m over its mean ground
  (200 over the highest any ground can be) until it is; nothing can be dug, placed or dropped there before. A ground
  that cannot be loaded sends you back to the spawn and /goto says so.
  `run=approach` (`run=leave`) films the way in from 3,000 km to 2 m over its day side (and out), `body=` another.
  `drop_home=1`: Terra and Luna stream by their size on screen too, as Mars does (off by default for now).
- Jupiter, the gas giant (generation 6; the name is a working one): `/goto jupiter` stands you on its cloud tops on
  the day side, 2.5 times Terra's pull. Gas has no blocks: nothing digs or places there, and the cloud tops hold
  you as a floor (the dive under them is not built). Fly up and round it as anywhere; its pull reaches 7.5 Mm.
  `run=stand body=3 eye=50000 look=jupiter` films it from 50 km over its cloud tops (`eye=` is the height over
  them). F3 lists it as "gas" under Size and "Feet in" says the cloud tops hold you. `report=1` gives every body's
  `gas`, `masses` (of Terra's) and `soi_m` under `interior`.
- Callisto, the giant's icy moon (generation 7; the name is a working one): `/goto callisto` waits for its ground
  ("going to Callisto: its ground is loading") and stands you on it. Its pull is light (1.85 m/s^2, a third of
  Luna's: high jumps); it keeps one face to the giant, which hangs 14.4 degrees wide and never rises or sets there;
  from the far side it is never seen. Its day is its orbit, 49 minutes of play. The ground: dark lag over dusty ice
  over clean ice on the old plains, bright ice in young craters and their rays, paler dusty ice on the flat middles
  of its two ringed basins, frost at the poles; nothing grows, no ore or caves yet. `run=stand body=4 at=-1,0,0
  look=jupiter` stands under the giant (`at=1,0,0` the far side); `report=1` prints a `callisto` section.
- Far bodies are points of light under a pixel (Mars, the giant, Terra from Mars, Luna), brightest at night,
  hidden by day and under the horizon: `run=stand time=900 look=Jupiter` (or `look=Mars`) faces one at night.
- Under the mantle's floor (layer 0 of a new world) lies the liquid outer core, a white-hot sea of iron and
  nickel: dig out a floor block and it glows up the hole, calm, at the floor's bottom; it holds you up like the
  floor did, but feet in it burn, a point of health every 0.1 s (survival; "<name> burned in the liquid metal").
- The aurora (0164-magnetic-field): curtains of green light with red-violet tops in a ring round each pole at
  night, about 70 degrees latitude at Terra's field; seen from the ground there and from orbit over the night side.
  `field=x` (the game) scales the home's magnetic field for the run (as core mining will lower it): the ring
  widens toward the equator and dims, at `field=0` there is none (held to 0..10; not a number reads as 1). The field is in F3 (World, Field) and in the
  run's JSON line (`field`).
