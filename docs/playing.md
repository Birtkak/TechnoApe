Moved out of CLAUDE.md (principle 15) so a feature's keys do not touch the file every session loads.

- Play: `technoape.exe run=play onscreen=1` (WASD, space; left click digs a block (in survival held, by its hardness, ADR 0034), right click places one (in survival with flint in hand, on rock, it strikes: 4 strikes knap a hand axe, `/give hand_axe`; the tool in hand digs its blocks in hardness / speed and wears out, ADR 0110; with the hand axe or a pick in hand right click on rock or ore prospects, once a second, a use each: the chat names the ore within its head's `prospect` metres, rich, strong, some or traces, or the rock rings empty; loose ore samples, nuggets and pebbles, lie over every body, taken by hand, ADR 0138; a thing on the ground (a stick, a stone, flint, a mushroom, berries, a sample) is aimed at by its own shape, nearer than the block behind it, and outlined on itself: left click takes it at once, right click held 0.3 s on it picks it up, draft-aim-at-things);
  H the build hammer, B its shape, wheel its height; V the bucket, left scoops, right pours;
  in space the mouse pitches you freely and Q/E roll, gravity rights you as it pulls (ADR 0024); C held zooms 3x;
  1-9 or the wheel the hotbar; Tab the inventory (ADR 0074, 0075: survival the hotbar and a worn pack's rows, a click lifts and puts, right click halves or puts one, shift-click moves across, outside throws a pile; closing the screen puts the cells back into the pack (the feet when full); a workbench or a crucible opens its own smaller window, not the inventory (ADR 0143): titled by the block, its part on top (the 3 x 3, or the crucible's slots, fire, heat and casts), the pack's rows and the hotbar under it, no ape, pile or Pack slot; Esc or Tab closes it; the recipe viewer stands docked at the right edge, opaque, whenever the inventory or a block's window is open (not in creative's grid): search, click an item for its pattern, what it makes and whether it needs the workbench (or where an item with no recipe comes from), Fill lays it out from the pack and draws red the cells you lack (dim at the 2 x 2 for a bench recipe, none at a crucible); anyone within 6 m may use it; dug, it drops all it holds), its fire's seconds, its heat as a number and a bar marked at what the work needs (1085 C for copper; it tops out at 1100), the work's arrow, and a row of casts to click (pick, axe, shovel, knife, hammer heads; again to clear); it lights a fuel only while it has work: 4 copper nuggets, 2 copper gravel or 1 malachite or chalcopyrite rubble melt to an ingot, ingots with a clay mould (two clay side by side) and a cast chosen make a head, the mould kept; a stick burns cool and short, a log hot enough, a coal lump hotter; the viewer shows melts and casts "in a crucible, at 1085 C" with no Fill; while it burns it plays the furnace model's fire, smoke, sound and light; acts `sslot:<k>` shift-clicks pack slot k, `cast:<name>` chooses a cast); tin and bronze (ADR 0142): the crucible has two In slots side by side (the mould now under Out; shift-click sends a stack to the first In that holds it or is empty, else the second); - Bots (`technoape-bot`): `prospect dx dy dz` strikes the prospecting tool in hand on that block and prints the answer
  as one JSON line, `{"bot":..,"prospect":[["copper","strong"],..]}` (empty: nothing in range); `hold k` first.
  Crafting as a player does: the grid's cells are slots 37 to 45 (a 3 x 3 row by row; 37, 38, 40, 41 the 2 x 2),
  filled by `move a b n`; `craft [n]` takes the output n times (1 if left out); `use dx dy dz` uses the block there
  (a workbench opens its 3 x 3), `close` closes the screen (the cells back into the pack); `slots` after a
  `wait 0.5` shows what came of it. A crucible (ADR 0141): `use dx dy dz` opens it, `put a dx dy dz k n` and
  `take dx dy dz k a n` at its cell reach its slots (0 in, 1 fuel, 2 mould, 3 out, 4 the second input for an alloy),
  `cast dx dy dz <name>` chooses a cast's shape by the name of any row of it (`cast dx dy dz` alone none), `furnace` prints the open one's slots, heat, fire and work as one JSON line.
