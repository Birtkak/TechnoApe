# TechnoApe – Lore (rudimentary)

Started 2026-10-27 by the developer: "the rest we will figure out later". Decisions in ADR 0098.

## What the player can learn

- **The Gelvins** are an alien race. Long ago they came to this world and planted a seed: an ape
  with an intellect gene. The player is one of these apes, an **apetion**.
- At the world spawn they left the **monolith**: two huge stone columns carved with glowing magenta
  runes, a seam of magenta light between them, on a stepped base in a ruined plaza. Nothing can break any
  of it. A beam of its light stands in the sky over the spawn, seen from anywhere. It watches the
  apetion and keeps a voxel record of it.
- **Death is not the end.** When an apetion dies its body crumbles to powder; the monolith rebuilds it
  from the record: the view breaks into green voxels, a Gelvin terminal reports `APETION 0007 SIGNAL LOST`, the cause, a
  reconstruction bar, and `APETION 0008 ONLINE. CONTINUE.` Every apetion gets a number, so the death
  count is the apetion's serial. The new body swirls together out of thin air, Gelvin magenta cooling
  to fur and lab coat. A bed is a beacon: the monolith rebuilds the apetion there instead
  of at spawn. What the dead one carried stays in its grave.
- The Gelvins can be obvious: their monolith, their terminal voice and their numbering are in plain
  sight from the first minute.

## What stays hidden

- The Gelvins did not plant the apetions out of kindness. They want them to climb all the way up the
  tech ages and build a **singularity** (probably the end-game item one day), and they want it for their
  own gain. The game never says this outright before the end. Hints may leak (a terminal line that
  says too much, a monolith that reacts to tech milestones), but the player is meant to find out late.

## Open (figure out later)

- What the Gelvins look like, where they went, and what they gain from the singularity.
- Whether other seeds, other apetions or other monoliths exist.
- How the progression eras (stone, metal, steam, electricity, ...) tie into Gelvin hints.
- Whether the monolith ever does anything besides rebuild (react to tech milestones, speak, open).
