# TechnoApe – Lore: the Indigenti and the awakened apes

- **Status:** living document, started 2026-11-16 in a brainstorm with the developer; reworked the same day
  after the developer settled how the Indigenti reached Terra. The story is the developer's; lines marked
  *proposal* are Claude's and open. Nothing here is built. It answers the open questions of ADR 0027 (the
  hostile creatures and the exotic resource; its corners call is dropped, ADR 0106) and would change
  `progression.md` (the fuel ages and two more systems); that rewrite waits until the story is settled.

**This file is the truth, not what the player is told.** The player learns most of it only on arrival at the third
system (see "How the story is told").

## The story in order
1. **The Indigenti** live at the second-closest star to Sol (name redacted, the developer's). They are
   advanced but cannot travel: the fuel for interstellar flight cannot be found, only made, and their system
   cannot make it (see below). So they work through small, slow, patient means.
2. **The swarm ship.** Long ago they sent a swarm in an unmanned ship driven by a **He-3 fusion engine**: slow
   (centuries, not decades), which fusion allows and a real interstellar fuel would not need.
3. **The crater.** At Terra the swarm dug **one crater from the surface to the core** and mined everything it
   needed there. The core's metal is the material of the next step.
4. **The monolith.** From the core's metal (*proposal:* an osmium-iridium alloy, the platinum-group metals that
   sink into a planet's iron core: very dense, very hard) it built a **huge monolith**. Its inside holds
   technology the ape will never understand.
5. **The awakening.** When the monolith was finished it **activated itself**. Its signals played on **biology
   the apes already had** (the developer, 2026-11-16: nothing was added to them) and their brains **evolved
   rapidly**: sentience, and an unstoppable hunger for advancement. The player's ape is one of them. Nobody touches anything; the
   monolith does this to every ape in reach at once.
6. **The swarm destroyed itself** in the crater, on purpose: a swarm that runs long enough rewrites its own
   code and turns hostile, so the Indigenti built it to end. Its scrap lies in the crater. The empty, partly
   destroyed **ship stays in orbit** around Terra.
7. **The wait.** The Indigenti wait for the species to become spacefaring and learn to make the fuel. Then they
   take over its free will, through the monolith, and make it their bottom-line supplier.

## The ape's drive is the plan
The hunger for advancement is why every need starts as a chore and ends as a factory, and why each age ends in
a megaproject (`progression.md`). The player is playing out the Indigenti's design. The ending is **left open
on purpose** (the developer, 2026-11-16): decided later, once the game shows what it has become. Nothing built
before then may assume one ending; the story's beats up to the third system must work with any of them.

## Setting: three systems
| # | System | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sol** (Terra, Luna, the planets) | where the game starts; the bulk of the early and mid game |
| 2 | **Alpha Centauri** | the nearest star; extra content; playtime to the moon is cut to make room |
| 3 | **The Indigenti's star** (name redacted) | their home; the end of the game |

## The fuel ladder
Each rung is a real kind of fuel, and each opens a new reach.

| Rung | Fuel | Reach | Where the ape finds it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Chemical** (hydrogen and oxygen, methane) | Luna and the nearer planets | EV in `progression.md` |
| 2 | **Fusion** (He-3 from Luna) | the outer solar system | **the swarm ship's broken He-3 engine** in orbit unlocks building it |
| 3 | **Antimatter** | Alpha Centauri, then the Indigenti's star | the last fuel; can only be made, never found |

### Why antimatter, and why the Indigenti cannot make it
Antimatter is stored power: real accelerators turn far less than a millionth of their input into it. A ship's
worth takes the whole grid of a planet-scale civilization, a collider ring and magnetic containment. The
Indigenti cannot make it, for three stacked reasons:
1. **Power.** Their star is a dim red dwarf and a flare star; flares wreck collectors.
2. **Materials.** Red dwarfs of that kind are metal-poor: small planets, no bulk metal, no superconductors or
   magnet material. Terra's core holds what they lack, which is why the swarm came.
3. **A past failure** (*proposal*): they tried. A containment accident destroyed their industrial world. The wreck
   is a late-game place, and shows what a failed collider does.

## How the story is told (the developer, 2026-11-16)
- **No direct confirmation of the Indigenti or their plan** before the third system. The player is never told who
  built the swarm, why, or what comes next.
- **What the player does have:** the monolith and its hum, the crater, the scrap, the wrecked ship, traces of other
  troops, and the ship's course, which points back to where it came from. These show that *something* was here, and
  the hunger for advancement makes the ape follow the question. The journal speaks in the ape's own words, as
  guesses and not facts (`journal.md`).
- **The reveal is arrival.** At the third system the player finds a metropolis, and the aliens make contact as the
  player flies toward the planet. How exactly, and what they say, is open on purpose. The sense of new content and
  of the unknown is the point.
- **Alpha Centauri** is the step in between: extra content, and no answer.

## Layout on Terra (the developer, 2026-11-16)
- **The crater is on the far side of the planet** (about 12 km from the start). The player never sees it early;
  it is found through the globe map or from orbit, so that part of the story waits until the player goes
  looking. Reaching it is a journey of its own (a long trek, later a railway), and once there, depth is its
  natural gate: heat, dark, pressure and the swarm's rogue remains grow with it.
- **The monolith is a few km from the start and 1 km tall** (the developer, 2026-11-16). On flat ground its tip
  shows over the horizon from about 3 km away (the horizon is about 120 m at eye height on a 4 km planet), higher
  from a hill, with a hum or a glow at night. It stands a quarter of the planet's radius high, well above the
  clouds (250 m) and the highest peaks (350 m), and casts a very long shadow at dusk. Its width and proportions
  are open. It pulls curiosity from the first hour without forcing the plot. The swarm built it far from its quarry; why
  (the Indigenti wanted it among the apes) is open.
- **The crater is a terraced bowl** (the developer, 2026-11-16): wide steps spiralling down to the core, like an
  open-pit mine, that can be walked or ridden the whole way. Each tier holds the swarm's work, its scrap and its
  dangers. **About 250 m across** (the developer, 2026-11-16). At 4 km deep that
  is a deep, narrow bowl: a walkable spiral at a 10 % grade turns about 50 times (each turn is 785 m long and drops
  about 78 m), a ramp some 40 km long, and the hole takes about 0.07 % of Terra's volume, so it costs little. *Proposal:* a wider ring of spoil around the
  rim so it reads from orbit. What lies at the bottom is open.
- **The crater has a raised rim of spoil**, like an impact crater (the developer, 2026-11-16), which keeps rivers and
  the counted water out of a hole that reaches the core. If the bake can manage it, the crater also stands on dry
  ground far from any river or lake, so the rim is the second line of defence, not the only one.
- **Luna has no story presence** (the developer, 2026-11-16): it holds the He-3 and the other resources of `worldgen.md` and nothing of the swarm's or the Indigenti's.
- Both are visible from orbit.

## What it gives the game
- **The crater** is a place to go: down through the rock, the ore and the heat to the core, past the swarm's
  scrap. It is the mid-game's big journey and is **visible from orbit**, like the monolith.
- **Scrap** is mined for **metal and for knowledge**. Knowledge arrives as noticed journal entries from
  examining it (`journal.md`), not as a research tree (ADR 0027).
- **The ship in orbit** is the first orbit's prize (EV age), and a **boardable wreck** (the developer, 2026-11-16):
  the player flies or climbs to it and explores its interior, with logs to read, a map and the broken He-3 engine.
  It is built from the same blocks and structures as everything else, in free space around Terra.
- **Hostile creatures stay in the crater** (the developer, 2026-11-16): pieces of the swarm that did not die and
  rewrote themselves. The danger is a place the player chooses to enter and grows with depth; the surface of Terra
  has only ordinary wildlife. They do not climb out and are not drawn by the player's factories (the other
  options in `open-questions.md` were set aside).
- **Antimatter later:** the collider ring is the fourth ring megaproject (after railway and power ring); the
  refinery of `progression.md` becomes the collider complex. Hydrogen comes from water, so it drains the seas; its
  power comes from Luna's fusion; the superconductor and magnet stuff comes from the core's metal. Antimatter
  cannot touch a wall, so its "pipes" are powered containment lines: if the power fails it annihilates and takes
  the facility with it.
- **One troop in play** (the developer, 2026-11-16: several living troops are too hard to build). The signal woke
  other troops too, but in play they appear only as **traces**: empty camps, tools and bones, computed from the seed
  like the clutter (`clutter-and-pickups.md`) and stored nowhere. If the AI troop is ever built (`open-questions.md`),
  the traces can become living troops without changing the story.
- **Multiplayer and the troop:** every awakened ape is the same troop, woken at once, which answers "are friends
  bound too" and fits the AI troop idea.

## What it needs from the engine (to spike before building)
- **A real core.** The developer wants the crater to reach the actual core (2026-11-16). Today the grid stops 64 m
  under the surface and the crust is 192 m (ADR 0003, ADR 0063); `world-data.md` ("Digging to the centre, kept open,
  not built") and `progression.md` ("Eating a planet") already call for deeper shells with fewer columns. This is the
  largest engine job in the story and needs its own spike and ADR.
- **Height for the monolith.** A body's grid ends at its build headroom (ADR 0021); a structure 1 km tall needs
  at least that above the ground, and far chunks must keep a thin 1 km structure visible from orbit (roadmap,
  "Big builds seen from afar": a far chunk point-samples edits). Both to check in the spike.
- **The ship's interior in orbit.** A body's grid ends at its build headroom (ADR 0021) and `roadmap.md` lists builds
  in free space as needing their own grid, as ships do. A boardable wreck is the first user of that grid, with its own
  gravity (none, or the ship's spin), air (none) and light. Needs a spike and an ADR before it is built.
- **The crater and monolith in the bake**, seen from orbit through the summary pyramid (`world-data.md`), on a
  planet that can be mined out later.
- **Scrap as counted ore bodies** in the ledger (ADR 0063), so metal from it stays exact.
- **An indestructible block.** The monolith is sealed and cannot be dug, entered or broken by any tool, to the
  end of the game (the developer, 2026-11-16: a permanent mystery). The engine needs a block or structure flagged
  unbreakable that the planet eater and every explosion skip, and the ledger counts it as fixed.

## Open questions
- **The ship:** small (the developer, 2026-11-16): a few minutes to explore, not a level. Open: how it is reached and boarded, and what it shows without confirming anything (damaged or unreadable logs, its course back to the origin star, the broken engine).
- **The monolith's later role:** its link is never explained directly (see "How the story is told"); what the player notices near it when the first antimatter is made is open.
- **What the player can do about the Indigenti:** left open on purpose (see above).
- **The Indigenti themselves:** how they look, how they speak, whether they are ever met.
- **Scope:** Alpha Centauri and the Indigenti's star as playable systems is a very large addition to the roadmap; the
  planet-sized content of `progression.md` (Starter to HV) stays the first target.
